# NewTube 1.9.0 release artwork

Final asset: `images/release_1.9.0.png` (1080×1350, portrait 4:5).

Mode: no image-generation tool was available in the release session
(2026-09-11), so the poster is a typographic composition rendered from a
hand-written HTML/CSS page and screenshotted with headless Chrome. There is no
caricature: the subject is the app itself (a phone showing the Downloads tab
with one finished and two in-progress cards, and download arrows) on the same navy stage / cream
lettering palette as the 1.8.0 Eugenio poster.

Text, exactly and only: "NewTube 1.9.0", "EDICIÓN CHIQUITO", "¡Te das cuen!",
"Ya se descargan.", footer "PARODIA · HOMENAJE NO OFICIAL". No percentages
beyond the illustrative card badges, no claims, no logos, no watermark.

It is fictional artwork and an unofficial homage to Chiquito de la Calzada,
not an endorsement.

NewTube is a free, open-source YouTube client for Android phones and tablets — an independent, unofficial fork of SmartTube (the Android TV client), rebuilt for touch.

It keeps SmartTube's battle-tested InnerTube engine and full feature set, and delivers a clean, calm, Material interface: a lean video feed, touch gestures, and a fast, minimal player.

Features:
• No ads — streams via YouTube's internal API, so there are no ads to block (no ad-blocker, no root)
• SponsorBlock — auto-skip sponsor segments (data © SponsorBlock contributors, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)
• DeArrow — de-clickbait titles and thumbnails (data © DeArrow contributors, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)
• Return YouTube Dislike
• Background playback and Picture-in-Picture
• Up to 4K / HDR with codec choice
• Optional sign-in with your real YouTube account (device-code flow)
• Open source (MIT), no analytics, no trackers

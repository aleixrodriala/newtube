## Validation and limitations

- Unit tests: **318 in `smarttubetv` and 70 in `common` passed**, no failures
  (10 new: rung selection, original-audio preference, size/duration/file-name
  formatting, Content-Range parsing).
- Downloads verified on the Pixel 9 (Android 17) with the debug build before
  the release build: 47-minute video at 144p and at 720p (the second replaced
  the first copy), audio-only of a short video into `Music/NewTube`, mid-transfer
  cancel from the card (registry entry and part files removed), progress badges
  and dimmed not-ready cards, playback of a finished card with no `/player`
  call, and airplane-mode cold start listing and playing the files plus the Home
  card falling back to the local copy.
- Signed release assembly passed. APK package/version and signing certificate
  checked with `aapt` and `apksigner`; the release is not debuggable.
- Pixel 9: in-place upgrade from the installed 1.8.1 build to the exact signed
  ARM64 artifact, app and account data retained, launch smoke check with the
  Downloads tab present.
- Full `lintStmobileRelease` was not re-run; the 1.8.0 record's pre-existing
  lint findings still stand.
- Known gaps: no pause/resume (cancel + retry, which resumes part files); no
  Wi-Fi-only or storage-location setting; no playlist downloads; subtitles not
  saved; no VP9/AV1 rungs above 1080p. Muxing a 300 MB file takes ~90 s on the
  Pixel 9 and shows as "Finishing…". YouTube can still refuse some videos and
  accounts; SABR stays off.

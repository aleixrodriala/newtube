SmartTube for phone

The SmartTube engine, made for your thumb.

SmartTube is the gold standard for ad-free YouTube on Android TV — but it's built for a remote. NewTube keeps its battle-tested InnerTube engine and full feature set, and rebuilds the interface for phones: portrait layouts, touch gestures, a fast minimal player.

Every other open-source phone client makes you choose — keep your account (ReVanced patches Google's app, with ban risk) or stay truly open (NewPipe, but no login). NewTube does both.

Built in the open with transparent, signed releases — engineered to be resilient and fast-updating as YouTube evolves.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is there a SmartTube version for Android phones?

SmartTube itself is Android-TV only. NewTube is an independent open-source fork that rebuilds SmartTube's ad-free YouTube engine for phones and tablets with a touch UI.

How do I watch YouTube without ads on Android without root?

Install NewTube's APK from GitHub Releases. It streams via YouTube's internal API, so ads are never delivered — no ad-blocker or root required.

Is NewTube free and open source?

Yes: free, MIT-licensed, no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases. Source and releases are on GitHub.

Does NewTube support background play and picture-in-picture?

Yes — background audio with lock-screen controls, plus Picture-in-Picture.

How is NewTube different from NewPipe, LibreTube, and ReVanced?

NewPipe and LibreTube can't sync your real YouTube account; ReVanced patches Google's proprietary app (ban/DMCA risk). NewTube keeps SmartTube's engine, is fully open source, and lets you optionally sign in with your real account.

Can I hide or disable YouTube Shorts?

Yes — the home is a clean single video grid, and sections like Shorts can be unpinned or hidden.

Does NewTube need a Google account, and does it track me?

No account required; sign-in is optional (to sync subscriptions, history and likes). NewTube has no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Does NewTube support SponsorBlock, 4K/HDR, and Return YouTube Dislike?

Yes to all: SponsorBlock (auto-skip), up to 4K/HDR, and real Return YouTube Dislike ratings.

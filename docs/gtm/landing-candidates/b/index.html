The one thing nobody else does

SmartTube for phone, made for your thumb.

SmartTube is the gold standard for ad-free YouTube on Android TV — but it's built for a remote. NewTube keeps its battle-tested InnerTube engine and full feature set, and rebuilds the interface for phones: portrait layouts, touch gestures, a fast minimal player.

Every other open-source phone client makes you choose — keep your account (ReVanced patches Google's app, with ban risk) or stay truly open (NewPipe, but no login). NewTube does both.

  • ✓ Keep your real YouTube account — optional, not required
  • ✓ No patching Google's app, no dead proxy servers
  • ✓ Open, transparent, signed builds — resilient and fast-updating

Zero interruptions

SponsorBlock in a YouTube app for Android.

Community-sourced SponsorBlock skips sponsor reads, intros, and self-promo automatically, with segment markers right on the seekbar. DeArrow quietly de-clickbaits titles and thumbnails, so your feed stops shouting at you.

  • Auto-skip sponsor, intro, and outro segments
  • Seekbar markers you can tap or override
  • DeArrow: honest titles, calmer thumbnails
Keep it playing

YouTube background play on Android — free.

Lock your phone and the audio keeps going, with full media and lock-screen controls. Pop any video into Picture-in-Picture to keep watching while you do something else. No Premium, no paywall.

  • Background audio with lock-screen controls
  • System Picture-in-Picture window
  • Play-in-background & screen-off from the overflow menu

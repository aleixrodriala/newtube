# NewTube — SEO Keyword Research (landing page + articles)

Pre-launch, no domain authority, no paid tools. Volumes/difficulty are
**qualitative** (high/med/low) with reasoning. Autocomplete terms harvested
from Google (`suggestqueries.google.com`) and DuckDuckGo (`duckduckgo.com/ac`)
suggestion endpoints; rankings verified by live search (July 2026).

---

## TL;DR strategy

Two facts shape everything:

1. **The wedge exists and nobody owns it.** SmartTube is a hugely popular
   ad-free TV app, and users clearly want it on phones — Google autocomplete
   returns *smarttube for phone / for android phone / for samsung phone /
   mobile / mobile version / mobile apk / alternative for phone*, and
   DuckDuckGo returns *smarttube android mobile*. But the **official SmartTube
   site literally says there is no phone version** and tells people to go use
   ReVanced / NewPipe / Pure Tuber. NewTube is a *fork of SmartTube rebuilt for
   phones* — we are the exact answer to a recurring, unmet, brand-intent query.
   This is our lowest-difficulty, highest-relevance cluster. Own it first.

2. **The head terms are high-competition.** "ad free youtube app android",
   "youtube without ads android", "youtube background play android free" are
   dominated by strong editorial domains (Android Authority, HowToGeek,
   Android Police, Brave) plus APK/listicle SEO farms and Play Store apps. We
   can't outrank those cold. Target them via *comparison/how-to articles* over
   time; don't bet the landing page on them.

**So:** landing page anchors on the winnable, high-relevance clusters
(SmartTube-for-phone, minimalist/no-Shorts, open-source client, no-root)
while still using the head term ("ad-free YouTube app for Android") as the
brand-defining phrase in the title. Articles chip at the harder head terms.

## Recommended landing page

**Primary keyword:** `ad-free YouTube app for Android` (brand-defining head
term), with the page **also engineered to win the winnable clusters**:
*SmartTube for phone / mobile*, *minimalist / no-Shorts YouTube client*,
*open-source YouTube app no root*.

**Secondary/anchor keyword (rank first):** `SmartTube for phone` /
`SmartTube mobile alternative`.

**Title tag (~62 chars):**
`NewTube — Ad-Free YouTube App for Android (No Root, Open Source)`

**Meta description (~155 chars):**
`NewTube is a free, open-source, ad-free YouTube client for Android phones — no root, no tracking. SponsorBlock, background play, PiP, 4K/HDR, no Shorts.`

**Alt title tag (to test, leads with the wedge):**
`NewTube — SmartTube for Your Phone: Ad-Free YouTube for Android`

## Method notes
- Autocomplete: `https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&q=...`
  and `https://duckduckgo.com/ac/?q=...` — both reachable, returned real terms.
- "Who ranks now" verified via live web search per cluster (July 2026).
- No numeric volumes invented; "volume" = breadth/recurrence of autocomplete
  variants + evergreen-ness of the pain point. Empty Google suggest (e.g.
  "youtube app no ads no root") = genuinely low volume but high purchase intent.
- Biggest strategic signal: SmartTube's own site turning phone users away with
  no product to send them to = an unmet, brand-qualified demand pool NewTube
  is uniquely positioned to capture.
